Version 1.8 brings a polished settings experience, more reliable reminders, and greater control over how todos are added.

New Features:
• Choose where new todos are added - set them to appear at the top or bottom of your Markdown files
• Optional Subtasks - enable or disable subtasks from settings to keep your lists simple
• Redesigned Settings - the settings screen now follows Material 3 guidelines, with compliant radio selection in choice dialogs

Improvements:
• More reliable due reminders - the reminder system was reworked to fire on time, even when the device is in the background
• Smoother todo swipe actions with a cleaner gesture handling approach
• The Daily Focus mode setting now only appears when Daily Focus is enabled

Under the hood:
• Updated Gradle, Android Gradle Plugin, and Compose BOM
• Removed unused code for a leaner app
