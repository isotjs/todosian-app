Version 2.2 brings a fresh Material 3 Expressive look across the app, with smoother animations and a cleaner settings screen.

New Features:
• Redesigned Settings - options are now grouped into cards with a large title that collapses as you scroll, and dependent options animate in as you enable them
• New empty states - the home, category, daily focus, and add-task screens now show friendly illustrated placeholders instead of plain text
• Redesigned onboarding - a clearer first-run screen with an animated hero and a prominent folder picker

Improvements:
• Smoother motion throughout - the app now uses the Material 3 Expressive motion scheme for consistent, more natural animations
• More expressive loading indicators while your files are being read
• Refreshed floating action buttons with a playful cookie shape

Under the hood:
• Updated Gradle, Android Gradle Plugin, Kotlin, Compose BOM, and AndroidX libraries
